Colour  Palette  
Primary  and  accent  colours  to  be  used  thoughtfully  (not  overpowering):  
●  Blue  –  trust,  professionalism  (primary  base  tone)  ●  Red  –  strength,  energy  (used  for  highlights/CTAs)  ●  Yellow  –  innovation,  attention  (subtle  accents  only)  ●  Grey/Black  –  premium,  industrial,  modern  base  
The  palette  should  feel  balanced  and  sophisticated,  not  overly  bright  or  cluttered.  
Visual  Style  &  Experience  
The  website  should  feel  alive,  dynamic,  and  interactive,  not  static.  
Key  elements  to  incorporate:  
●  Subtle  animations  and  motion  effects  ●  Scroll-based  interactions  (e.g.,  sections  revealing  as  you  scroll)  ●  Horizontal/scrollwheel  galleries  for  projects  ●  Parallax  or  layered  movement  for  depth  ●  Smooth  transitions  between  sections  
Interactive  Inspiration  
●  Moving  or  animated  elements  (e.g.,  a  rotating  globe  or  similar  visual  anchor)  ●  Dynamic  project  showcases  instead  of  static  image  grids  ●  Engaging  hero  section  with  motion  (video  or  animated  visuals  preferred)  
Design  Approach  
●  Avoid  rigid,  boxy/grid-heavy  layouts  ●  Focus  on  fluid,  immersive  sections  ●  Use  full-width  visuals  and  layered  content  ●  Prioritise  a  high-end,  cinematic  feel
